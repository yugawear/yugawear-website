At YUGA, our products are made through a print-on-demand process. Each product is produced specifically after an order is placed.

This model helps us avoid unnecessary inventory and reduce overproduction and waste.

Because our products are made to order, we cannot offer unrestricted returns for change of mind or incorrect size selection. However, if we make a mistake or you receive a product that is defective, damaged, misprinted, or different from what you ordered, we will work with you to make it right.

Our goal is to keep this policy fair to both our customers and our small, growing brand.

01

WHEN CAN YOU REQUEST A RETURN OR REPLACEMENT?

You may contact YUGA if you receive a product that is:

  • Damaged during delivery
  • Defective
  • Misprinted
  • Significantly different from the product shown or ordered
  • The wrong product
  • The wrong size due to a fulfillment error
  • Missing from your order

Eligible issues will be reviewed individually based on the evidence provided.

If the issue is confirmed to be a manufacturing, printing, fulfillment, or delivery-related problem, YUGA may provide a replacement or refund, depending on the circumstances.

02

REPORT THE ISSUE WITHIN 7 DAYS

CLAIM WINDOW 7 DAYS FROM DELIVERY

Please contact us within 7 days of delivery if there is a problem with your order.

Requests submitted after 7 days may not be eligible for a free replacement or refund because it becomes significantly more difficult to determine when and how the issue occurred.

However, if applicable law provides you with a longer or different right, those rights will continue to apply.

03

HOW TO REQUEST A RETURN / REPLACEMENT

Please email us at:

yugawear.in@gmail.com

with the following information:

  • Order ID
  • Name used for the order
  • Contact details
  • Description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the product
  • Clear photographs of the affected area
  • Photograph of the packaging, where relevant
  • Unboxing video, if available

For printing or manufacturing issues, please provide photographs showing the issue clearly.

For size-related issues, we may request photographs of the product along with measurements so that we can compare them with the applicable size chart.

Unboxing video recommended

An unboxing video is strongly recommended, especially for damaged or incorrect orders, because it helps us and our fulfillment partner determine what happened during delivery.

However, the absence of an unboxing video does not automatically mean that your claim will be rejected.

We will review the information available and assess the claim fairly.

04

DAMAGED PRODUCTS

If your package or product arrives damaged:

  1. Take photographs of the package before opening it, if possible.
  2. Record an unboxing video if possible.
  3. Take clear photographs of the damaged product.
  4. Contact YUGA within 7 days of delivery.

If the damage is determined to have occurred during fulfillment or transportation, YUGA may provide a replacement or refund as appropriate.

If the product is damaged by the customer after delivery, the damage will generally not qualify for a replacement or refund.

05

DEFECTIVE OR MISPRINTED PRODUCTS

If your product has a genuine manufacturing or printing defect, please contact us within 7 days of delivery.

Examples may include:

  • Major printing defects
  • Incorrect artwork
  • Severe print placement issues
  • Printing that is materially different from the product ordered
  • Major stitching or garment defects
  • Incorrect product supplied

Minor variations that naturally occur during garment manufacturing or digital printing may not qualify as defects.

OPTION 01

Replacement

A replacement product may be produced and shipped.

OPTION 02

Refund

A refund may be issued where replacement is not reasonably possible or appropriate.

The final resolution will depend on the nature of the issue and product availability.

06

WRONG PRODUCT

If you receive a product that is different from the product you ordered, please contact us within 7 days.

After verification, YUGA will arrange an appropriate resolution, which may include:

  • Replacement with the correct product
  • Refund, where replacement is not reasonably possible

Where the error is caused by YUGA or our fulfillment process, the customer will not be required to bear the cost of correcting the error.

07

WRONG SIZE SELECTED BY CUSTOMER

Please check the size chart before ordering.

Because YUGA products are produced specifically after an order is placed, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges when the customer selects the wrong size.

For example, if you order a size L and later decide that you needed an XL, the order will generally not qualify for a return or free exchange.

This policy exists because the product has already been specifically produced for that order.

If you are unsure about your size, please contact us before placing your order and we will be happy to help where possible.

08

SIZE MEASUREMENT TOLERANCE

±0.5" APPROXIMATE NORMAL MANUFACTURING TOLERANCE

Garment measurements can vary slightly during manufacturing.

A measurement variation of approximately ±0.5 inch may occur and is considered a normal manufacturing tolerance for apparel.

Such minor variation will generally not qualify as a manufacturing defect.

If you believe your product has a significant measurement discrepancy, please contact us with photographs and measurements so that we can review the issue.

09

CHANGE OF MIND

We currently do not accept returns or refunds simply because:

  • You changed your mind
  • You no longer want the product
  • You ordered the wrong size
  • You don't like the colour after receiving it
  • You expected the product to look different despite it matching the product description
  • You found another product you prefer

Because YUGA uses print-on-demand production, products are generally made specifically for individual orders.

This helps us reduce unnecessary inventory and overproduction, but it also means we cannot treat every order like conventional stocked merchandise.

10

COLOUR AND PRINT VARIATIONS

Digital displays can show colours differently depending on the device, screen, brightness, and settings used.

Additionally, digital garment printing may produce slight differences between the artwork displayed on a screen and the final printed garment.

Minor colour differences or subtle print variations do not automatically qualify as defects.

However, if the product is materially different from what was ordered, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

11

HYGIENE AND USED PRODUCTS

For hygiene and product-quality reasons, products that have been:

  • Worn
  • Washed
  • Ironed improperly
  • Altered
  • Damaged after delivery
  • Stained
  • Exposed to chemicals
  • Modified in any way by the customer

will generally not qualify for return or replacement unless the issue is determined to be an inherent manufacturing defect.

Please inspect your product before wearing or washing it.
12

RETURN SHIPPING

If the product is confirmed to be defective, damaged, misprinted, or incorrectly fulfilled due to an error attributable to YUGA or our fulfillment process, YUGA will determine the appropriate return/replacement arrangement.

Customers will generally not be asked to bear return costs for an error caused by YUGA.

For customer-requested returns that are not covered by this policy, YUGA may decline the return because our products are made to order.

13

RETURN-TO-ORIGIN (RTO)

An order may be returned to the fulfillment facility when delivery cannot be completed.

Common reasons include:

  • Incorrect address
  • Incomplete address
  • Incorrect PIN code
  • Customer unavailable
  • Customer refusing delivery
  • Customer not responding to the courier
  • PIN code becoming unserviceable
  • Other customer-related delivery issues

If an order is returned to origin because of information or actions attributable to the customer, YUGA may offer reshipment subject to applicable shipping or fulfillment charges.

If the issue is caused by YUGA or our fulfillment process, we will review the situation and provide an appropriate resolution.

14

LOST IN TRANSIT

If an order is confirmed by the shipping partner as lost during transit, YUGA will work with the fulfillment and shipping partner to resolve the issue.

Depending on the circumstances, we may provide:

  • A replacement shipment
  • A refund

The resolution will be initiated once the shipment is reasonably confirmed as lost by the relevant shipping/logistics provider.

15

DELIVERY DELAYS

A delayed delivery does not automatically mean that an order is lost.

If your order is delayed, please first check the tracking information provided to you.

If the shipment remains unreasonably delayed or the shipping partner confirms that it is lost, please contact us.

We will investigate the shipment and work toward an appropriate resolution.

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or other rights that may apply under applicable law where goods are not delivered within the agreed or legally applicable timeframe.

16

REFUND PROCESS

01 Request reviewed
02 Refund approved
03 Refund initiated
04 Bank/payment provider processes it

If your refund request is approved, the refund will generally be processed using the original payment method, where technically and legally possible.

Depending on the payment provider and banking institution, the amount may take additional time to appear in your account after the refund has been initiated.

YUGA is not responsible for delays caused solely by banks, payment gateways, or other financial institutions after the refund has been initiated.

17

REFUND AMOUNT

Where a refund is approved because of a defect, damage, incorrect product, or fulfillment error attributable to YUGA, the refund will be determined based on the applicable order and circumstances.

Where required by applicable law, applicable shipping charges and other amounts will also be handled accordingly.

We will communicate the applicable refund amount before processing the refund wherever reasonably possible.

18

PARTIAL REFUNDS

A partial refund may be considered where:

  • Only part of an order is affected
  • The issue is limited to a specific product
  • The customer agrees to retain a product with a minor issue
  • Applicable circumstances justify a partial resolution

Partial refunds will be offered only where appropriate and will not affect any rights available under applicable law.

19

PROMOTIONAL AND DISCOUNTED ORDERS

Products purchased using promotional offers, discount codes, or special campaigns remain subject to this Return & Refund Policy.

A discount does not remove a customer's rights in relation to defective, damaged, incorrect, or otherwise non-conforming products.

20

CASH ON DELIVERY ORDERS

Where Cash on Delivery is available, customers are responsible for accepting and receiving their orders.

Refusing or repeatedly failing to accept a COD order may result in the order being returned to origin.

Future COD availability may be restricted where there is a history of repeated refused or unaccepted orders.

This does not affect any rights relating to defective or incorrectly supplied products.

21

FRAUDULENT OR ABUSIVE CLAIMS

YUGA aims to handle every genuine customer complaint fairly.

However, we may investigate claims where there is reasonable evidence of:

  • Repeated fraudulent refund requests
  • Intentional product damage
  • False information
  • Manipulation of evidence
  • Abuse of replacement/refund policies
  • Other fraudulent activity

Where a claim cannot reasonably be verified, we may request additional information before making a decision.

Nothing in this section is intended to restrict legitimate consumer rights.

22

OUR APPROACH TO RETURNS

YUGA is a small, growing brand and operates using a print-on-demand model.

We do not hold large quantities of ready-made inventory.

When you place an order, your product is produced specifically for you.

This model helps us:

  • Reduce overproduction
  • Avoid unnecessary inventory
  • Reduce fashion waste
  • Produce only what is ordered
  • Build YUGA sustainably as we grow
OUR PROMISE Sustainability should never be an excuse for ignoring a genuine customer problem.

If we make a mistake, we will take responsibility and work toward a fair resolution.

23

POLICY AND CONSUMER RIGHTS

This Return & Refund Policy is intended to explain YUGA's standard return and refund process.

It does not exclude, restrict, or replace any rights or remedies that a consumer may have under applicable Indian laws.

Where applicable law provides a consumer with a right that differs from this policy, the applicable legal right will prevail.
