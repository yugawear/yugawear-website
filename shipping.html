At YUGA, every product is made and prepared for you through our print-on-demand fulfillment process. We aim to process and deliver every order as quickly and safely as possible.

01

FREE SHIPPING ACROSS INDIA

We offer Free Standard Shipping across India on all eligible YUGA orders.

There are no additional standard shipping charges at checkout for eligible orders.

02

ORDER PROCESSING

PROCESSING TIME 2–4 business days

Once your order is successfully placed, it is sent for production and fulfillment.

Processing includes:

  • Order verification
  • Product production
  • Printing
  • Quality checking
  • Packing
  • Handover to the shipping partner

Orders are generally processed within 2–4 business days.

Processing time does not include the delivery time.

During high-volume periods, product launches, sales, holidays, or unforeseen operational circumstances, processing may take longer than usual.

03

DELIVERY TIME

ESTIMATED DELIVERY 3–7 business days after dispatch

Once your order has been processed and handed over to the shipping partner, delivery generally takes approximately 3–7 business days, depending on your location and PIN code.

Delivery times may vary due to:

  • Destination PIN code
  • Courier serviceability
  • Weather conditions
  • Public holidays
  • Regional disruptions
  • Courier delays
  • Remote or difficult-to-service locations
  • Other circumstances beyond YUGA's reasonable control

Delivery timelines are estimates and are not guaranteed delivery dates.

Some remote locations may require additional delivery time.

04

TOTAL ESTIMATED DELIVERY TIME

For most orders:

PROCESSING 2–4 business days
DELIVERY 3–7 business days
ESTIMATED TOTAL TIME Approximately 5–11 business days

The delivery period begins after the order has completed processing and has been handed over to the shipping partner.

05

ORDER TRACKING

Your order is trackable.

Once your order has been shipped, tracking details will be shared with you through the contact information provided during checkout.

You can use the tracking information to view the current status and movement of your shipment.

Please allow some time for the tracking information to become active after the shipment is handed over to the courier.

06

DELIVERY ATTEMPTS

Our shipping partners may make multiple delivery attempts if the recipient is unavailable.

Please ensure that:

  • Your delivery address is complete and accurate.
  • Your PIN code is correct.
  • Your mobile number is active.
  • Someone is available to receive the package when required.
07

INCORRECT ADDRESS OR DELIVERY INFORMATION

YUGA is not responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete information provided by the customer.

This includes:

  • Incorrect address
  • Incorrect PIN code
  • Incorrect mobile number
  • Missing address information
  • Customer being unavailable
  • Customer refusing delivery

If an order is returned to the fulfillment facility because of customer-related delivery issues, additional shipping or reshipping charges may apply.

08

RETURN TO ORIGIN (RTO)

An order may be returned to the sender if delivery cannot be completed.

Possible reasons include:

  • Customer unavailable
  • Incorrect or incomplete address
  • Customer refusal
  • Incorrect PIN code
  • Courier unable to reach the delivery location
  • Other delivery-related issues

If an order is returned because of circumstances attributable to the customer, YUGA may require the customer to pay applicable reshipping charges before the order can be shipped again.

If the RTO occurs because of an error attributable to YUGA or the fulfillment process, we will review the situation and provide an appropriate resolution.

09

DELAYED DELIVERY

Although we work with fulfillment and logistics partners to deliver orders within the estimated timeframe, delivery delays may occasionally occur.

YUGA is not responsible for delays caused by circumstances beyond our reasonable control, including:

  • Severe weather
  • Natural disasters
  • Public emergencies
  • Courier disruptions
  • Transportation disruptions
  • Government restrictions
  • Strikes
  • Regional disruptions
  • Technical or infrastructure failures

If your order is significantly delayed, please contact us at:

yugawear.in@gmail.com

We will make reasonable efforts to assist you.

10

REMOTE AND SPECIAL SERVICE LOCATIONS

Orders shipped to remote areas, difficult-to-service PIN codes, or locations with limited courier connectivity may require additional delivery time.

In certain cases, alternative shipping partners may be used to complete delivery.

11

DAMAGED PACKAGE

If your package appears damaged, opened, or tampered with when delivered, please document the condition of the package before opening it.

We strongly recommend recording a continuous unboxing video from the moment the package is received until the product is fully removed from the packaging.

If the product inside is damaged, defective, misprinted, or incorrect, please contact us as soon as possible with:

  • Order ID
  • Clear photographs of the package
  • Clear photographs of the product
  • Photographs/videos showing the issue
  • Unboxing video, where available

Please refer to our Return & Refund Policy for eligibility and claim procedures.
