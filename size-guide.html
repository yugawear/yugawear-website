How to Find Your Size

The easiest way to choose your YUGA size is to compare it with a T-shirt you already own and like.

01

Lay it flat

Place a well-fitting T-shirt on a flat surface without stretching the fabric.

02

Measure the chest

Measure straight across from one armpit to the other.

03

Measure the length

Measure from the highest shoulder point beside the collar to the bottom hem.

04

Compare

Compare your measurements with the YUGA chart and choose the closest size.
